# Warranty Vault

Ever bought headphones, found a fault three weeks later, and couldn't remember whether you could still return them? Or needed a serial number for an insurance claim and spent an evening digging through folders?

Warranty Vault reads the receipts, order emails, manuals and serial-label photos that are already on your UGREEN NAS, and gives you one list of what you own:

- what you paid and where,
- the serial number,
- the last day you can return it,
- when the warranty ends,
- and which file proves each of those things.

Try saying:

- "What do I own that's still under warranty?"
- "Can I still return anything I bought this month?"
- "Build a warranty register from my Receipts and Downloads folders and save it."

It only reads until you say yes. Saving the register and tagging the source files each ask you first. It never contacts shops, never files claims, and never sends your documents anywhere.
